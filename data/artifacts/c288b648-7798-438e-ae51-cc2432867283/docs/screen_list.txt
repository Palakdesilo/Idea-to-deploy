Here is the wireframe for a one-page personal/professional profile:

**Page Structure:**

1. **Header**
2. **Sidebar**
3. **Main Content Area**
	* **Grid Section**
	* **Form Section**
4. **Footer**

**Wireframe Descriptions:**

**Header (150px x 50px)**

* A horizontal bar at the top of the page with the following elements:
	+ Logo (30px x 20px) on the left
	+ Navigation menu (20px x 20px) with links to About, Skills, Experience, and Contact
	+ Profile name (max 15 characters) on the right

**Sidebar (250px x 1000px)**

* A vertical bar on the left side of the page with the following elements:
	+ **About Me** section (150px x 50px) with a brief summary of who you are
	+ **Skills** list (100px x 20px) with bullet points and icons for each skill
	+ **Experience** timeline (200px x 20px) with job titles, dates, and company logos

**Main Content Area**

* A grid section that takes up most of the page:
	+ **Grid Section** (800px x 500px):
		- 4 columns, each containing a different piece of information about you:
			1. **Headline**: A short summary of your professional brand
			2. **Summary**: A brief overview of your experience and skills
			3. **Achievements**: A list of notable achievements or awards
			4. **Quote**: A motivational quote or a statement about your values

* A form section below the grid:
	+ **Form Section** (300px x 100px):
		- A simple contact form with fields for name, email, and message
		- A submit button to send the form

**Footer**

* A horizontal bar at the bottom of the page with the following elements:
	+ Copyright information (max 20 characters)
	+ Social media links (icons only) for LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook, and Instagram
