### Structural Component-Based Wireframe Descriptions

I will describe the layout and functional elements for each page using common structural components like Header, Sidebar, Grid, Form, etc. No visual styling (colors, shadows, 3D) will be included.

**Common Components Glossary:**
*   **Header:** Top horizontal strip, global navigation.
*   **Footer:** Bottom horizontal strip, global links, copyright.
*   **Navigation Menu:** List of links.
*   **Search Bar:** Input field for searching.
*   **Hero Section:** Large, prominent area for banners/promotions.
*   **Product Grid:** Layout displaying multiple product cards.
*   **Product Card:** Block for a single product (image, name, price, CTA).
*   **Filter/Sort Controls:** UI elements to refine lists.
*   **Form:** Collection of input fields and buttons.
*   **CTA Button:** Call to Action button.
*   **Breadcrumbs:** Navigation path.
*   **Pagination:** Controls for multi-page results.
*   **Tabbed Content:** Area with multiple selectable tabs.

---

#### 1. Homepage

*   **Header (Top Horizontal Strip):**
    *   **Left:** Logo (Brand Name/Image).
    *   **Center:** Main Navigation Menu (e.g., "Shop", "Categories", "Deals", "About Us").
    *   **Right:** Search Bar (input field with search icon/button), User Account Icon/Link, Shopping Cart Icon (with item count).
*   **Main Content Area:**
    *   **Hero Section (Full Width Banner):** Large rectangular area for a prominent promotional image, headline, and a Call to Action (CTA) button.
    *   **Featured Categories Section (Grid Layout):** A section title (e.g., "Shop by Category"). Below it, a grid of 3-4 large rectangular blocks, each with an image, category name, and a link.
    *   **New Arrivals / Bestsellers Section (Product Grid):** A section title (e.g., "New Arrivals"). Below it, a horizontal scrollable row or a grid of 4-6 Product Cards. Each Product Card includes: Product Image, Product Name, Price, and an "Add to Cart" button.
    *   **Promotional Banner Section (Horizontal Strip):** A narrower full-width banner for a secondary promotion or announcement.
    *   **Testimonials / Brand Story Section (Text Blocks):** A section title. Contains 2-3 text blocks with quotes/short paragraphs and author names.
*   **Footer (Bottom Horizontal Strip):**
    *   **Left:** Copyright information, Brand Logo (optional).
    *   **Center:** Quick Links (e.g., "About Us", "Contact", "FAQ", "Privacy Policy").
    *   **Right:** Social Media Icons, Newsletter Signup Form (email input + subscribe button).

#### 2. Product Listing Page (Category/Search Results)

*   **Header (Top Horizontal Strip):** (Same as Homepage)
*   **Main Content Area:**
    *   **Breadcrumbs (Top Left):** Navigation path (e.g., "Home > Electronics > Smartphones").
    *   **Page Title (Top Center):** H1 heading for the current category or search query.
    *   **Left Sidebar (Vertical Strip):**
        *   **Category Navigation:** List of subcategories or related categories.
        *   **Filter Options:** Collapsible sections for various filters (e.g., Price Range Slider, Checkboxes for Brand, Color, Size, Rating). Each filter has a label and interactive elements.
    *   **Right Main Content (Grid Layout):**
        *   **Sort By Controls (Top Right):** Dropdown menu for sorting products (e.g., "Price: Low to High", "Newest").
        *   **Product Grid:** A grid of Product Cards (3-4 columns). Each Product Card includes: Product Image, Product Name, Price, Rating (optional), and an "Add to Cart" button.
        *   **Pagination Controls (Bottom Center):** Numbered links or "Previous/Next" buttons.
*   **Footer (Bottom Horizontal Strip):** (Same as Homepage)

#### 3. Product Detail Page

*   **Header (Top Horizontal Strip):** (Same as Homepage)
*   **Main Content Area:**
    *   **Breadcrumbs (Top Left):** Navigation path (e.g., "Home > Electronics > Smartphones > iPhone 15").
    *   **Main Product Section (Two-Column Layout):**
        *   **Left Column (Product Media):**
            *   **Large Product Image:** Main display area.
            *   **Thumbnail Gallery (Below/Side):** Smaller images/thumbnails.
        *   **Right Column (Product Information & Actions):**
            *   **Product Name (H1):** Large heading.
            *   **Brand Name (Text):** Smaller text.
            *   **Price (Prominent Text):** Clearly displayed current price.
            *   **Rating & Reviews Link (Text/Stars):** Visual rating and a link.
            *   **Short Description (Text Block):** Brief overview.
            *   **Variant Selectors (Dropdowns/Radio Buttons):** Options for size, color, etc.
            *   **Quantity Selector (Input Field with +/- buttons):** To choose number of items.
            *   **Add to Cart Button (Prominent CTA):** Large button.
            *   **Buy Now Button (Secondary CTA, optional):** Direct purchase option.
            *   **Availability Status (Text):** "In Stock" or "Out of Stock".
            *   **Delivery Information (Text):** Estimated delivery time.
            *   **Wishlist Button (Icon/Text):** To add to a wishlist.
    *   **Product Details & Reviews Section (Tabbed Content):**
        *   **Tabs:** "Description", "Specifications", "Reviews", "Q&A".
        *   **Content Area:** Displays detailed information based on the selected tab.
    *   **Related Products Section (Horizontal Scroll/Grid):** A section title (e.g., "Customers Also Viewed"). Below it, a horizontal scrollable row or a grid of 3-4 Product Cards.
*   **Footer (Bottom Horizontal Strip):** (Same as Homepage)

#### 4. Shopping Cart Page

*   **Header (Top Horizontal Strip):** (Same as Homepage)
*   **Main Content Area:**
    *   **Page Title (H1):** "Shopping Cart" or "Your Cart".
    *   **Cart Items Section (List Layout):**
        *   **Table/List Header:** Columns for "Product", "Price", "Quantity", "Subtotal", "Remove".
        *   **Each Cart Item Row:** Product Image (small), Product Name (link), Unit Price, Quantity Selector, Item Subtotal, Remove Button/Icon.
    *   **Cart Actions (Below Item List):**
        *   **Continue Shopping Button:** Link back to a product listing page.
        *   **Update Cart Button:** To apply quantity changes.
    *   **Order Summary Section (Right Sidebar or Below Cart Items):**
        *   **Section Title:** "Order Summary".
        *   **Subtotal:** Total price of all items.
        *   **Shipping Cost:** Estimated or "Calculated at Checkout".
        *   **Tax:** Estimated or "Calculated at Checkout".
        *   **Discount Code Input:** Input field and "Apply" button.
        *   **Total (Prominent Text):** Final amount.
        *   **Checkout Button (Prominent CTA):** Large button to proceed to checkout.
*   **Footer (Bottom Horizontal Strip):** (Same as Homepage)

#### 5. Checkout Page (Simplified - One Page Checkout)

*   **Header (Top Horizontal Strip):** (Simplified Header: Logo, "Checkout" text, no search/cart icons).
*   **Main Content Area:**
    *   **Page Title (H1):** "Checkout".
    *   **Checkout Progress Indicator (Top Center):** Visual steps (e.g., "Shipping > Payment > Review").
    *   **Two-Column Layout:**
        *   **Left Column (Shipping & Payment Forms):**
            *   **Shipping Information Section:** Section Title "Shipping Address", Form Fields (Name, Address, City, State, Zip, Country, Phone, Email), "Use as Billing Address" Checkbox.
            *   **Shipping Method Selector:** Radio buttons for options (e.g., Standard, Express) with costs.
            *   **Payment Information Section:** Section Title "Payment Method", Payment Options (Radio buttons for "Credit Card", "PayPal"), Credit Card Form (if selected: Card Number, Expiry, CVV, Cardholder Name), Billing Address Form (if different).
        *   **Right Column (Order Summary & Place Order):**
            *   **Order Summary Section:** Section Title "Your Order", List of Items (Name, Quantity, Price), Subtotal, Shipping, Tax, Total (Prominent).
            *   **Terms & Conditions Checkbox:** "I agree to the Terms and Conditions".
            *   **Place Order Button (Prominent CTA):** Large button to finalize purchase.
*   **Footer (Bottom Horizontal Strip):** (Simplified Footer: Copyright, Privacy Policy link).

#### 6. Login / Register Page

*   **Header (Top Horizontal Strip):** (Simplified Header: Logo, "My Account" text).
*   **Main Content Area (Centered Layout):**
    *   **Page Title (H1):** "My Account" or "Login / Register".
    *   **Two-Column Form Layout (or Tabbed/Toggle):**
        *   **Left Column (Login Form):** Section Title "Login", Form Fields (Email, Password), "Remember Me" Checkbox, Forgot Password Link, Login Button (CTA), Social Login Buttons (optional: Google, Facebook).
        *   **Right Column (Register Form):** Section Title "Create an Account", Form Fields (First Name, Last Name, Email, Password, Confirm Password), "Subscribe to Newsletter" Checkbox (optional), Register Button (CTA).
*   **Footer (Bottom Horizontal Strip):** (Same as Homepage)
